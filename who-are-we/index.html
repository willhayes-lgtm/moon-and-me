Who Are We?

Moon & Me Toys & Books began when our little girl became fascinated with the CBeebies programme. We found it difficult to track down character toys and realised that other parents might be searching for them too.

We built this independent guide to keep Moon & Me toys and books together in one place. We are not a toy retailer. Instead, we link visitors to retailers such as Amazon, and may earn a commission when a qualifying purchase is made.
