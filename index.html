About the Moon & Me television series

Created by Andrew Davenport, Moon & Me follows a group of toy-house friends whose adventures begin after dark. The characters include Pepi Nana, Mr Onion, Lambkin, Dibillo, Colly Wobble, Little Nana, Lily Plant and Moon Baby.
