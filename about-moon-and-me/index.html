About Moon & Me

Moon & Me is a children’s television series created by Andrew Davenport. Its story takes place in a toy house, where the friends enjoy imaginative adventures after dark.

Meet Pepi Nana, Mr Onion, Lambkin, Dibillo, Colly Wobble, Little Nana, Lily Plant and Moon Baby. The original Moon & Me Toys & Books website shared guides to character toys, books and gifts inspired by the programme.

Moon & Me stories

Some early episodes listed on the original website include Pepi Nana’s Letter, The Big Tea, Everybody in the Tub!, Humpty Onion and The Silly Song.
